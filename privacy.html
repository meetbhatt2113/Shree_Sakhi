Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Shree Sakhi exists to help girls and women ask sensitive questions without fear. That only works if we're genuinely careful with your information. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, what we don't, and why.

What we never collect

  • Your name (unless you choose to type one into an optional field)
  • Your exact location (the "Find a Doctor" tool opens Google Maps in a new tab — we never see or store your address)
  • Voice recordings on our website server. Speech recognition is handled by your browser or device and may use its speech service; check your browser's privacy settings.
  • Any tracking cookies for advertising

What we do collect, and why

Tracker data (cycle dates, pregnancy week, symptoms you tick): this is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data or using a different device/browser will reset it.

Text of your questions to Sakhi AI: when you ask a question (by voice or typing), the text is sent to a third-party AI service to generate a reply. The website does not offer question saving or chat history. Avoid including identifying information in your question. Service providers may process request metadata under their own policies.

Human questions and callbacks: we currently do not accept questions for human review or callback requests through this website. The AI chat is automated and is not monitored as a help inbox.

Third parties involved

To make certain features work, some information passes through these services:

  • AI and hosting services: our service providers process the text of your AI question to generate a response and deliver the website. Do not put your name, phone number, or address in chat messages.
  • Google Maps: if you use "Find a Doctor," your search opens directly in Google Maps in a new tab — this is subject to Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

Your choices

  • You can use almost the entire site — FAQs, care guides, myths, tools — without submitting any personal information at all.
  • You can clear your tracker data at any time using the "Clear history" / "Reset" buttons within each tool.
  • You can use the site without creating an account. Avoid identifying details when asking the AI a question.

Children's privacy

We know younger girls may use this site for first-period information. There is no account or contact form. Questions entered in AI chat are sent to the providers described above, so please do not include identifying details. Parents and guardians should guide younger users when appropriate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions?

We are not currently operating a staffed support inbox. Please do not send privacy requests through the AI chat.
