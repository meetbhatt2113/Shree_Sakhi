Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Shree Sakhi exists to help girls and women ask sensitive questions without fear. That only works if we're genuinely careful with your information. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, what we don't, and why.

What Shree Sakhi does not store

  • Chat questions or chat history for future use.
  • Your exact location through the "Find a Doctor" tool. Its search opens Google Maps in a new tab.
  • Voice recordings for future use. If you use the mic, a short recording is sent through our service to an external transcription service to turn speech into text, then discarded by our service after processing.

How site features use information

Tracker data (cycle dates, pregnancy week, symptoms you tick): this is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data or using a different device/browser will reset it.

Voice and text questions to Sakhi AI: Shree Sakhi does not save your questions, voice recordings, or chat history for future use, and we do not sell your information. If you use the mic, a short recording is sent to an external speech transcription service; the resulting text is sent to an external AI service to generate a reply. If you play a generated voice reply, its text is also sent to that voice service. Avoid including identifying information in your question. Service providers may process request metadata under their own policies.

Human questions and callbacks: we currently do not accept questions for human review or callback requests through this website. The AI chat is automated and is not monitored as a help inbox.

Advertising and cookies

Current status: Shree Sakhi does not currently display Google AdSense ads or load its advertising script. The advertising information below describes what will apply if we enable ads in the future; we will update this page and any required consent choices when that happens.

If Google ads are enabled: third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. Ad partners may process information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and page visits under their own privacy policies. Advertising would be separate from the Sakhi AI chat; we will not intentionally send your chat questions or local tracker entries to ad partners as ad targeting data.

Your choices: you can manage Google's personalized ads in My Ad Center. You can also learn about third-party ad personalization choices at AboutAds or NAI opt-out. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, although some ads may still appear and opt-out choices may depend on cookies.

If we serve ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, we will use an appropriate consent management platform to show required choices before using advertising cookies where consent is required. Ads may be limited when consent is not given.

Third parties involved

To make certain features work, some information passes through these services:

  • AI and hosting services: our service providers process the text of your AI question to generate a response and deliver the website. Do not put your name, phone number, or address in chat messages.
  • Google Maps: if you use "Find a Doctor," your search opens directly in Google Maps in a new tab — this is subject to Google's own privacy policy, not ours.
  • Advertising partners, if ads are enabled: Google and other participating ad vendors may use the technologies and data described in the advertising section above. See Google's advertising privacy information.

Your choices

  • You can use almost the entire site — FAQs, care guides, myths, tools — without submitting any personal information at all.
  • You can clear your tracker data at any time using the "Clear history" / "Reset" buttons within each tool.
  • You can use the site without creating an account. Avoid identifying details when asking the AI a question.

Children's privacy

We know younger girls may use this site for first-period information. There is no account or contact form. Questions entered in AI chat are sent to the providers described above, so please do not include identifying details. If we enable ads, we will review age-related ad settings and avoid using sensitive health questions for ad targeting. Parents and guardians should guide younger users when appropriate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page. When consent is required for advertising cookies, we will request it through a separate choice instead of treating continued use as consent.

Questions?

We are not currently operating a staffed support inbox. Please do not send privacy requests through the AI chat.
